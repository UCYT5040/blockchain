# Play as a trial player

If you don't have Minecraft yet, you can play as a trial player.

After **6h** of work, you will be able to earn Minecraft and play as a full player.

## Setup your editor

Check out our [quickstart guide](quickstart) to set up your editor. Come back here when you're ready to start coding.

## Open the simulator

Navigate to the [simualtor](https://www.craftos-pc.cc/online/).

When you want to test your code, drag and drop your `.lua` file into the simulator. You can then run it by typing its name into the console (e.g., `hello_world`).

> **⚠️ Warning:** Time spent programming in the simulator's console is not tracked by Hackatime and thus **does not count** towards your 6h of work. You must write your code in a file in your own editor and drag it into the simulator to test it.

## Project guidelines

Since trial players do not have the ability to interact with the various in-game peripherals, your projects will be simpler. Simple graphics or text-based programs are a great place to start.
